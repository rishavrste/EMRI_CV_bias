NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* HEADLINE -- THE ECCENTRICITY BIAS DID NOT GO AWAY.  In ABSOLUTE terms e0 moves
  from 1.1091e-3 to 1.0564e-3, i.e. it shrinks by 4.8%.  The best fit is still
  e0 = 0.038944 against an injected 0.040.  What changes is the error bar:
  sigma(e0) inflates from 2.603e-4 to 1.428e-3 (5.5x) once the two deviation
  parameters are marginalised, so the SAME bias reads as 0.74 sigma instead of
  4.26 sigma.  Granting the PN deviation DILUTES the statement; it does not
  de-bias the eccentricity.

* The same pattern holds across the board.  Every bias/sigma improves, but four
  of the six absolute biases move by less than 25%, and m2's absolute bias gets
  4.1x WORSE while its bias/sigma "improves" from +0.170 to +0.027 -- purely
  because sigma(m2) inflates 26x (0.281 -> 7.276).  Read the absolute column,
  not the scaled one, when asking whether the deviation recovers the truth.

* The deviation DOES buy a genuinely better fit: mismatch 4.72x lower, and the
  class-swap control above says that is all disk physics.  The disk is absorbed
  mainly through the ECCENTRICITY channel of the deviation: C_e = 3.060 while
  C_p = 0.406.  C_p is nowhere near the -36.9 / +64 values that recur in the
  vacuum-vs-1PA EMRI grid, so this is a different absorption direction.

* CONTROL DEFECT in the +-5 sigma box -- worth fixing before the next run.  The
  Fisher sigma for dev_2 evaluated at deviation = 0 is 6.63e-3, giving a box of
  +-0.0331, while the converged answer is C_e = 3.06 -- about 460 sigma outside
  its own search box.  (sigma(dev_1) = 11.3 vs sigma(dev_2) = 6.6e-3 is a factor
  1700 anisotropy.)  DE consequently exhausted all 1100 iterations still at
  0.9828 and the UNCONSTRAINED LM did the entire climb to 0.999998.  A fixed
  deviation box (the +-100 guard already in the config) is the right prescription
  here, not a Fisher-scaled one.

* NM escape was not used; LM stopped at iteration 134 with "no damped step lowers
  chi2".  LM alone moved the mismatch from 1.72e-2 to 1.99e-6.
