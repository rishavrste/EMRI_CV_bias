NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The disk torque is absorbed almost entirely into ECCENTRICITY: e0 is biased by
  -4.26 sigma.  Everything else is sub-sigma except p0 at +0.84 sigma.  The vacuum
  template fits the perturbed signal to a mismatch of 9.4e-6, but pays for it with
  a four-sigma shift in e0.  That is the systematic bias for this source.

* CAVEAT -- the recovered e0 sits 0.57 sigma inside the lower wall of the +-5 sigma
  box (0.0388909 vs the floor 0.0387471).  It is NOT truncated: DE found an
  interior minimum and LM's lambda ran away to 2.5e138, confirming no step
  improves it.  But a wider box (sigma_range 8) is a cheap check on whether the
  minimum wants to travel further.

* The LM stage moved the overlap by 9e-11 -- expected, since DE ran with
  polish=True and L-BFGS-B had already converged on the winner.  The lambda
  explosion is the confirmation, not a failure.

* Seeding from the injection is hopeless here on its own: overlap at the injected
  parameters is 0.0234 (chi2 1.42e4) from ~22 rad of accumulated dephasing.  A
  local climber started there has nothing to follow; the population search is
  what crosses the ridge.
